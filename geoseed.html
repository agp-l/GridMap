OpSec Protocol Guidelines

  • Environment: Run this tool entirely offline. Do not connect to the internet.
  • Mobile Usage: Do not use standard operating systems. It is highly recommended to use a hardened OS like GrapheneOS for executing this securely on mobile devices.
  • Desktop Usage: Boot an ephemeral operating system (e.g., Tails OS, Live Ubuntu) from a USB flash drive. Delete the environment completely after seed generation.
  • Geographic Entropy: Do NOT choose locations close to each other. Locations must be globally distributed to maximize cryptographic entropy.
  • Passphrase (BIP39 Extension): If your chosen locations share any geographical proximity, you MUST secure your hardware wallet with a strong Passphrase (at least 13 completely random characters or 7 random words).
